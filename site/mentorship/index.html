Mentorship

You weren't meant to build alone.

A 30-day mentorship that pairs you with a trained mentor who walks beside you, calls out what's possible, and helps you lead yourself and your family well.

Apply now

30 days

Focused and finite

A clear first season with a written plan, so the growth lasts after the 30 days end.

Virtual

Wherever you are

You meet with your mentor online, so distance and schedule don't keep you out.

Free to you

Covered for you

Once your application is approved, your mentorship is fully sponsored. Cost should never be the reason a man stays stuck.

Who it's for

Men who are ready to stop drifting.

Husbands, fathers and leaders who are present but distracted, responsible but drifting, and ready for change but unsure where to begin.

You don't need to have it figured out. You need to be honest about where you are and willing to do the work.

How it works

Four steps

  1. 1

    Apply

    Tell us where you are and where you want to go. It takes about ten minutes.

  2. 2

    Review

    Our team reads every application and follows up personally.

  3. 3

    Match

    Approved men are matched with a trained mentor.

  4. 4

    Meet

    Your first virtual meeting sets your 30-day plan.
