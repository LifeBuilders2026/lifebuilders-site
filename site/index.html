Why we exist

Drifting men create broken families.

Men are increasingly isolated, lacking mentors, drifting without purpose, and struggling to lead themselves, their families and their communities. The result is fractured homes, fatherlessness, addiction, loneliness, and cycles that pass from one generation to the next.

Many men want help. Cost keeps them from it. Life Builders exists to change that.

What we do

Three ways we build men

The Standard Podcast

A message that gives language to the drift

The Standard with Preston Woody brings practical, faith-based teaching on life, leadership, marriage and growth to men wherever they listen.

Bible Experience

Truth that becomes a daily rhythm

A reading and listening plan built to help men experience the entire Bible, ten to fifteen minutes a day, and live what they read.

Mentorship

One man's journey

How one man moves from drift to generational impact

Meet Daniel: 38, husband, father of two. Present, but distracted. Responsible, but drifting. Ready for change, but unsure where to begin.

  1. 1 · Hears

    The message

    The Standard gives language to his drift. "I don't have to stay here."

  2. 2 · Roots

    In truth

    The Bible plan moves truth into his daily rhythm.

  3. 3 · Responds

    Takes ownership

    He completes an assessment, then applies for mentorship.

  4. 4 · Accesses

    A path forward

    A scholarship removes the financial barrier. A mentor walks beside him.

  5. 5 · Transforms

    Becomes a leader

    Faith and discipline deepen. His family sees the change.

Daniel is an illustration. Your story can follow the same path.
